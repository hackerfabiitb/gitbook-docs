# Litho performance

## Resolution test patterns

Pitch: the spacing between corresponding points of an array. Known in math as the period:

$$
T: f(x+T=f(x)
$$

A line array with 50% duty cycle is a pattern with linewidth=x, spacewidth=x, and period=x+x=2x



Isolated lines take spacewidth>>linewidth giving enough room for underexposed tails to extend
